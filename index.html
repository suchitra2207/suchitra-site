research

My research looks at how people and technology shape each other, from the chatbots we lean on every day to women’s place in an AI-driven job market.

From Assistance to Dependence: The Growing Influence of Chatbots in Human Life

My first published research paper, on how chatbots have moved from helpful tools to a growing presence in daily life.

Published in Iconic Research and Engineering Journals, Volume 9, Issue 5, November 2025.

read the paper

From Digital Inclusion to AI Inclusion: Women, Generative AI, and the Future of Work in India and South Korea

A comparative, desk-based study of how India and South Korea can advance women’s inclusion in AI-driven work. It proposes four dimensions of AI inclusion (digital access, AI capability, workplace opportunity and institutional support) and five actions linked to India–Korea cooperation on AI skills, research, policy and industry.

Among the sources I reviewed, none directly measures women’s AI inclusion in either country. The paper treats that gap as a finding in itself.

Presented at the India–Korea International Conference 2026 (IKIC 2026), organised by the India-Korea Forum, online, 23 to 25 October 2026.
